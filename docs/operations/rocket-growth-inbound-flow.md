# Rocket Growth Inbound Flow

## Positioning
Use “Rocket Growth inbound preparation/support” until operational authority is verified. Do not claim guaranteed acceptance, official partnership, customs clearance, KC certification, or delivery outcome guarantees.

## Customer inputs
- Target sales channel and platform intent (e.g. Coupang Rocket Growth, Smartstore).
- Product SKU, barcode, and packaging requirements.
- Product dimensions, weight, carton/pallet configuration.
- Labeling, barcode, and packaging rules.
- Desired quantity and inbound timing.
- Compliance or document requirements if applicable.

## Operator tasks
- Receive goods at the China warehouse and verify quantities.
- Perform inspection/photo evidence checks for quality and count.
- Apply labeling, barcode, and packaging work per inbound rules.
- Prepare cartons, pallets, affidavits, or packing lists for inbound shipping.
- Coordinate customs/shipping documentation where applicable.
- Update inbound preparation evidence and next-step guidance.

## Rocket Growth inbound flow states
- `china_warehouse_received` — goods received in the China warehouse.
- `inspection_running` — inspection and quality/photo evidence are being collected.
- `labeling_packaging` — labeling, barcode, and packaging work is in progress.
- `rocket_growth_inbound_prep` — inbound preparation checklist and coordination are underway.
- `customs_shipping` — customs/shipping coordination is in progress.
- `inbound_complete` — inbound preparation evidence has been completed.

## Evidence requirements
- `china_warehouse_received`: receipt counts, warehouse photos, and confirmation of received SKUs.
- `inspection_running`: inspection checklist, defect notes, and photo evidence.
- `labeling_packaging`: label/barcode application notes and packaging photos.
- `rocket_growth_inbound_prep`: inbound prep checklist, packing list, and coordination notes.
- `customs_shipping`: customs/shipping document status or coordination notes.
- `inbound_complete`: final handover evidence and inbound readiness confirmation.

## Boundary guidance
- Position this flow as coordination and preparation, not a guarantee of Rocket Growth admission.
- Do not infer Rocket Growth approval from inspection or packaging completion alone.

## Risk note
- Keep operational claims descriptive and evidence-based.
- Avoid any statement that implies the platform will accept inbound shipments without separate business confirmation.
