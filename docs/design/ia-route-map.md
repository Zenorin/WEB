# IA and Route Map

## Public routes
- `/` — Home: landing page that introduces the renewed NEEDS TRADE service model and primary quote CTA.
- `/services` — Services overview for sourcing, OEM/ODM, production, inspection, compliance, and Rocket Growth inbound preparation.
- `/services/sourcing` — China sourcing & purchase agency service details.
- `/services/oem-odm` — OEM/ODM and product development service details.
- `/services/production` — Goods production / manufacturing coordination details.
- `/services/inspection-packaging` — Inspection, labeling, barcode, packaging, and photo evidence details.
- `/services/compliance` — Compliance support boundaries, KC/origin/customs readiness, and disclaimer guidance.
- `/services/rocket-growth` — Rocket Growth inbound preparation and coordination support.
- `/portfolio` — Portfolio / case examples using evidence-safe content and service summaries.
- `/fee-guide` — Fee guide block and service estimate model.
- `/faq` — Frequently asked questions and inquiry guidance.
- `/quote` — Quote request intake for product URL, idea, Excel, sample, or inbound preparation.

## Workspace routes
- `/workspace` — Customer workspace overview of active requests and statuses.
- `/workspace/requests/:requestId` — Request detail page with status timeline, evidence, and next steps.

## Admin routes
- `/admin` — Admin operations dashboard shell for internal request triage.
- `/admin/requests/:requestId` — Admin request detail and status update / evidence upload placeholder.

## Route purpose summary
- Home: conversion-focused summary of service categories and a quote/inquiry CTA.
- Services: deeper explanation of each service without making unsupported guarantee claims.
- Quote: request intake contract and validation boundaries.
- Workspace: customer-facing progress tracking and evidence presentation.
- Admin: internal status management shell and operator workflow placeholder.
- Portfolio/Fee Guide/FAQ: trust, transparency, and service clarity.
