## Observed page roles
- Landing/home page establishes a China sourcing and OEM/ODM renewal proposition.
- Service pages explain sourcing, OEM, inspection, compliance, and inbound preparation support.
- Quote intake path captures product details, service intent, contact information, and request type.
- Evidence and trust sections emphasize operational steps, inspection photos, and preparation checklists.
- Customer/workspace and admin management are implied through future request tracking sections.

## Section hierarchy and conversion triggers
- Hero with primary service promise and quote request CTA.
- Service category cards for sourcing, OEM/ODM, goods production, inspection/packaging, compliance, and Rocket Growth inbound preparation.
- Process timeline or flow chart to set expectations.
- Quote request form or inquiry trigger near top and repeated after service details.
- Trust/evidence blocks showing inspection, packaging, photo evidence, and customer support signals.
- FAQ and contact guidance for reassurance and next-step clarity.

## Trust signals and evidence patterns
- Operation steps or a timeline with clearly labeled stages.
- Photo evidence and inspection support language.
- Checklists for preparation, packaging, and inbound readiness.
- Support/contact information and inquiry encouragement.

## Inferred requirements
- Present NEEDS TRADE as a one-stop renewal for China sourcing through inbound preparation, not a finished marketplace.
- Build a quote intake flow that supports product URL inquiry, bulk inquiry, OEM samples, inspection evidence requests, and Rocket Growth prep.
- Keep customer workspace and admin workspace boundaries explicit.
- Use reference pages as role and service structure input, not as a source for exact copy or assets.
- Defer extension/collector automation until separate approval.
- Avoid claims about customs clearance, KC certification, Coupang approval, or delivery guarantees.

## Clean-room reject list
- Copied source: do not reuse HTML, CSS, JavaScript, class names, or internal structure from reference pages.
- Copied layout text: do not reuse exact slogans, headings, or branded copy.
- Copied assets: do not reuse images, logos, icons, screenshots, or branded visuals.
- Tracking code: do not reuse analytics pixels, embedded scripts, or tag manager snippets.
- Cookies/sessions: do not reuse hidden cookie management, session flows, or login mechanisms.
- Credentials: do not reuse any real credentials, API keys, tokens, or scraped login details.

## Role decision
- Renew NEEDS TRADE as a clean-room service platform with clarity on quote intake and operation status.
- Frame Rocket Growth as inbound preparation support, not a guaranteed platform approval.
- Keep extension, collectors, and automated supplier crawling out of the first scope.
