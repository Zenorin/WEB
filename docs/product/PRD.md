# NEEDS TRADE Renewal PRD

## Product goal
Build a conversion-first website and future operations workspace for China sourcing, purchase agency, OEM/ODM, goods production, inspection, labeling/packaging, compliance support, warehouse handling, and Coupang Rocket Growth inbound preparation.

## Target users
- Coupang and Smartstore sellers sourcing products from China.
- Brand owners planning OEM/ODM or goods production.
- Companies ordering promotional goods.
- Operators managing quotes, samples, production, inspection, and inbound evidence.

## Non-goals
- No copied competitor code, assets, images, icons, slogans, tracking snippets, or exact marketing copy.
- No hidden credential/session handling.
- No claim of regulated service responsibility unless confirmed.
- No full marketplace or ERP in the first slice.

## Core flows
1. Visitor understands the one-stop service model.
2. Visitor submits product URL, product idea, or Excel bulk request.
3. System records quote request and status.
4. Operator reviews and updates quote/status/evidence.
5. Customer sees progress in workspace.

## Active-now scope
- Main landing route.
- Service route shell.
- Quote request form contract.
- Customer workspace shell.
- Admin operations shell placeholder.
- Clean-room reference role report.

## Deferred-with-contract scope
- Chrome extension or collector automation.
- 1688 API or scraping integration.
- Payment integration.
- Production-grade file storage.
- Automated customer notifications.
