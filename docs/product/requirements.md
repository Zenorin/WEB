# Requirements

## Functional requirements
- Public landing page explains the one-stop China sourcing/OEM/logistics service.
- Service pages separate sourcing, OEM/ODM, goods production, inspection/packaging, compliance support, warehouse handling, and Rocket Growth inbound preparation.
- Quote request form accepts URL, idea, quantity, sales channel, required services, and attachment metadata.
- Customer workspace shell displays request list, status timeline, quote summary, evidence placeholders, blocker notices, and next actions.
- Admin shell is guarded and may remain scaffold-only until operations requirements are confirmed.
- Operations statuses are shared by contracts, API, web, workspace, and admin.

## Security and privacy requirements
- No real credentials, cookies, sessions, platform login data, customer passwords, API keys, or tokens in repo files.
- `.env.example` must contain placeholders only.
- Reference pages may not contribute copied source, assets, or exact copy.
