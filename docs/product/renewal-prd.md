# NEEDS TRADE Renewal PRD

## Product goal
Build a clean-room renewal website and operation planning package for NEEDS TRADE as an integrated China sourcing, OEM/ODM, inspection, customs support, warehouse handling, and Coupang Rocket Growth inbound preparation platform.

## Business interpretation
- Treat NEEDS TRADE as a renewal target, not a finished product.
- Use the reference pages only as service-flow, structure, and customer journey guidance.
- Position Rocket Growth as China-side inbound preparation and coordination support, not a guaranteed platform approval.
- Keep the first implementation focused on web renewal, quote intake, and clear customer/admin workspace boundaries.

## Target users
- Coupang and Smartstore sellers importing or preparing inventory from China.
- Corporate buyers ordering promotional goods or bulk merchandise.
- Brand starters planning OEM/ODM product development.
- China sourcing operators handling supplier discovery, quality checks, and inbound preparation.
- Internal operators who manage quotes, samples, inspections, and status tracking.

## Core user journeys
1. Product URL inquiry
   - Visitor submits a product URL or product concept for sourcing guidance.
   - System documents source link, product details, target channel, and required services.
2. Bulk Excel inquiry
   - Visitor uploads or describes bulk product lists and quantity/packaging requirements.
   - System captures batch inquiry details for supplier sourcing and quote estimation.
3. OEM sample request
   - Visitor requests OEM/ODM sample work, material guidance, and production estimates.
   - System records sample specs, requested quantities, and expected delivery checkpoints.
4. Rocket Growth inbound request
   - Visitor requests Coupang Rocket Growth inbound preparation and support.
   - System captures warehouse, labeling, carton, barcode, and shipment coordination needs.
5. Inspection/photo evidence request
   - Visitor requests inspection and photo evidence for product quality and packaging.
   - System tracks inspection evidence requirements and operator notes.
6. Ongoing status tracking
   - Customer views request progress through workspace status cards and evidence updates.
   - Operator updates lifecycle stages, blockers, and next action guidance.

## Service categories
- China sourcing / purchase agency
- OEM/ODM product development
- Goods production / manufacturing coordination
- Inspection, labeling, barcode, and packaging support
- Customs and compliance readiness guidance
- Rocket Growth inbound preparation and evidence support

## Non-goals
- No hidden login/session handling or backend credential storage in this slice.
- No unauthorized crawling, browser extension scraping, or 1688 automation without separate approval.
- No platform guarantee claims for Coupang, customs clearance, KC certification, or delivery outcomes.
- No copied competitor content, exact layout wording, or branded assets from the reference sites.
- No full marketplace, ERP, or payment processing in the first implementation.

## Success criteria
- Public landing and service pages define a clear renewal target and service scope.
- Quote intake contract is documented and validated.
- Customer workspace and admin workspace routes are scoped with clear boundaries.
- Reference report documents observed facts, inferred requirements, and open questions.
- Extension, collectors, and scraping remain deferred and explicitly gated.

## Defer and guard
- Chrome extension / browser collector features remain deferred.
- 1688 crawling or direct supplier scraping remains outside the first slice.
- Automated payment or notification integration remains outside the first slice.
- Storage of sensitive user credentials or API keys remains forbidden.
