# Rocket Growth Inbound Flow

## Use when
- Writing Rocket Growth service copy, route content, status contracts, or operations flow.
- Connecting China warehouse work to Coupang inbound preparation.

## Correct framing
- Treat this as China-side inbound preparation unless the user verifies direct contractual/logistics capability.
- Model work such as quantity check, inspection photo, barcode/label, carton/box information, pallet or shipment preparation, documentation, and inbound evidence.
- Do not claim guaranteed Coupang acceptance, official Coupang partnership, customs agency status, or direct 3PL replacement unless verified.

## Required workflow
1. List required customer inputs: product info, channel, SKU/barcode, inbound plan, carton data, compliance documents, target timeline.
2. List operator tasks: warehouse receipt, inspection, label/barcode, box/pallet prep, shipment handoff, evidence upload.
3. Map tasks to quote-intake statuses.
4. Write public copy carefully: “입고 준비/대행/지원” rather than unsupported guarantees.
5. Flag platform-policy, compliance, customs, and responsibility assumptions.

## Handoff
- Report changed files, commands run, PASS/FAIL results, remaining risks, rollback note, and personal input needs.
